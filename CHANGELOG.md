## 0.0.1

* Initial release of `network_speed_gauge`.
* Futuristic circular speedometer dial & bandwidth meter using high-performance `CustomPainter`.
* Dynamic needle pointer with realistic spring / elastic damping physics responding to live bandwidth values.
* Multi-threshold neon speed arcs (Low, Medium, High, Ultra-fast) with customizable gradient sweeps and glow effects.
* Integrated digital readouts: central animated speed counter and sub-metrics for Download, Upload, Ping, and Jitter.
* Intelligent auto-scaling unit formatting between Kbps, Mbps, and Gbps.
* Custom tick marks, major/minor subdivisions, needle geometries, and configurable arc sweep angles (180°, 240°, 270°).
